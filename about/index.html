About Us

Ancaster's Homegrown Team for Heating & Cooling

From the stone cottages along Wilson Street to the newer builds of the Meadowlands, our licensed technicians keep homes across Ancaster and the Hamilton-Wentworth region comfortable through every Niagara Escarpment winter and humid lakeside summer.

Our Story

Rooted in Ancaster, Trusted Across Hamilton-Wentworth

At Ancaster Heating & Cooling, we treat every service call as a chance to look after a neighbour. Our shop sits on Wilson Street East in the heart of old Ancaster, and the homes we care for stretch from the historic limestone houses near the Escarpment to the growing subdivisions of Rousseaux, Summit Park, and the Meadowlands.

Living in the shadow of the Niagara Escarpment means real seasons, bitter cold snaps that sweep down through the Dundas Valley, the occasional ice storm that tests older wiring, and sticky July weeks when humidity off Lake Ontario settles over the community. That climate mix shows up in the housing stock too: 19th-century stone homes with ductwork challenges all their own, mid-century bungalows in the older pockets, and tightly built modern two-storeys that demand high-efficiency equipment. We know how each of those housing types responds to an Ontario winter, because we have worked inside all of them.

Our day-to-day work covers the full scope of residential comfort. We handle emergency furnace repair on the coldest January morning, central and ductless air conditioning for humid August afternoons, cold-climate heat pumps for homeowners moving off fossil heat, smart thermostat upgrades, complete duct cleaning that clears years of dust and pollen, and fireplace installations that turn a living room into a proper gathering place. When a heritage home near Sulphur Springs Road needs cooling without ducts, a ductless mini-split is usually the right answer; when a new build in Binbrook needs a furnace, we start with a real load calculation rather than a rule of thumb.

As a family-run company with deep ties to Ancaster and the wider Hamilton area, we have built our name one honest quote at a time. We will not oversell equipment a homeowner does not need, and we will always explain what is wrong, what it costs to fix, and what the options are before any work begins. That is the standard we would want for our own families, and it is the standard we bring to every call in Dundas, Stoney Creek, Waterdown, Binbrook, Mount Hope, and Hamilton.

The Work We Handle Across Ancaster Every Week

A typical week sends us up on the Escarpment for a no-heat call on an older forced-air furnace, into a Meadowlands two-storey for a central air tune-up, and out to a country property near Mount Hope or Binbrook to commission a new cold-climate heat pump. We install and service gas furnaces, central and ductless air conditioners, heat pumps, gas and electric fireplaces, smart thermostats, and complete duct cleaning, residential and light-commercial, across all the brands common in Ontario homes.

Our Commitment

Our Commitment to Ancaster and Hamilton-Wentworth Homeowners

Every system we install is sized to the actual home, not a rule of thumb. That matters more in Ancaster than in many places, because the microclimate along the Escarpment and the wide mix of housing, from poorly insulated 1800s stone to tightly sealed modern construction, means a furnace or heat pump that is perfect for one house can be wildly wrong for the one next door. Proper load calculations, clean ductwork, and correctly matched equipment are how we keep energy bills reasonable and systems running for the long haul.

When you call (905) 304-7182, you reach a local team that lives in and around the community, not a call centre. We show up in the window we promise, lay out pricing before any work starts, and treat your home the way we would treat our own, drop cloths down, boots off or covered where it makes sense, and a tidy cleanup before we leave. It is old-fashioned service, and customers across Hamilton-Wentworth tell us it is what keeps them coming back.
